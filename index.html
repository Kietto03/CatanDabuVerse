📜 Catan Game Rules & Costs

Victory Condition: Reach the target Victory Points limit to win the match (Cities give 2 VP, Settlements 1 VP, Victory Point Development Cards count hiddenly).

Setup Phase: Each player places 1 settlement and 1 adjacent road (forward turn order, then reverse turn order). In the second setup step, you receive starting resources bordering your placed settlement.

Gameplay Loop: Roll the dice to distribute resources. If a 7 is rolled, the Robber is activated, players with >7 cards discard half, and the current player moves the Robber to steal a card. Build roads, settlements, cities, or buy dev cards.

🛠️ Construction Costs:

  • Road/Ship: 🌲 1 Wood, 🧱 1 Brick
  • Settlement: 🌲 1 Wood, 🧱 1 Brick, 🌾 1 Wheat, 🐑 1 Sheep
  • City (Upgrade): ⛰️ 3 Ore, 🌾 2 Wheat
  • Development Card: ⛰️ 1 Ore, 🌾 1 Wheat, 🐑 1 Sheep
